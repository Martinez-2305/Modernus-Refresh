All Services Provided

Interior & Exterior Painting

We offer professional painting services for both the inside and outside of your home or property. Whether you're looking to refresh a single room or give your entire exterior a makeover, we use high-quality paints and precise techniques for a smooth, lasting finish. Our team ensures clean lines, full coverage, and minimal disruption.

Wallpaper Hanging

Add style and personality to any room with our expert wallpaper hanging service. From feature walls to full-room coverage, we ensure seamless alignment and bubble-free application. We work with all wallpaper types, including textured and patterned designs — delivering a perfect, professional finish every time.

Decorative Finishes

Give your walls a unique touch with our range of decorative finishes. We apply techniques like sponging, rag rolling, and textured effects to create custom looks that elevate your space. Ideal for accent walls or special rooms that deserve a standout finish crafted with skill and creativity.

Woodwork & Finishing

From skirting boards and architraves to doors and bespoke woodwork, we deliver crisp, clean painted finishes that complete the overall look of any space. Our attention to preparation and priming ensures a durable, professional result that stands the test of time.

Spray Painting

Using modern airless spray equipment, we achieve a factory-smooth, even finish across large surfaces, cabinetry, and furniture. Our hybrid approach combines the speed and precision of spray technology with traditional finishing for outstanding results on any scale of project.

Our Quality Guarantee

At Modernus, we are committed to delivering exceptional craftsmanship and attention to detail in every project we take on. We use only high-quality materials and professional techniques to ensure a flawless and durable finish.

Your satisfaction is at the heart of what we do — if you're not completely happy, we'll work with you until it's right. That's the Modernus promise.
