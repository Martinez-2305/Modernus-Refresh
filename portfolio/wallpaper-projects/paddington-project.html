About This Project

A bespoke wallpaper installation in a stylish Paddington apartment

This project involved the full wallpaper installation across multiple rooms of a modern apartment in Paddington, W2. Our team carefully prepared all surfaces before hanging a premium designer wallpaper, ensuring perfectly aligned seams, bubble-free application, and crisp finishes around every architrave and corner.

Wallpaper hanging demands precision and patience — two things our team take great pride in. The finished result transformed the space, adding character and depth that paint alone could not achieve.
