About This Project

A full wallpaper installation across a family home in Putney

This project covered the wallpaper hanging throughout a family home in Putney, SW15. Our team carried out full surface preparation before fitting a combination of patterned and textured wallpapers, carefully matching repeat patterns across every drop for a seamless, professional result.

From feature walls to full room installations, the client wanted a home that felt distinctly personal and elevated. We delivered a finish that exceeded expectations — clean edges, tight seams, and not a bubble in sight.
